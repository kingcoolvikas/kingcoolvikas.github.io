ABOUT ME

I'm a Security Analyst and AppSec VAPT Specialist based in Patna, Bihar, India. With hands-on expertise across web applications, APIs, Android, and network security, I bring a deeply offensive mindset to defensive security.

I've completed 50+ VAPT engagements, uncovering 300+ vulnerabilities across government and private-sector clients — including 40+ government organizations. I also actively participate in bug bounty programs, having reported valid vulnerabilities to over 100 companies globally.

Outside assessments, I contribute to the security community through conference talks, labs, and open-source tooling. I believe in clarity in security reporting and building real security maturity — not just checkbox compliance.
